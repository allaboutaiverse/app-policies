QR Studio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how the QR Studio Android app ("the app") handles information. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to a minimum.

Camera

The app uses your camera only to scan QR codes and barcodes on your device. Camera images are processed on-device and are never uploaded, stored or shared.

Your data

Your scan and create history is stored locally on your device only. We do not run servers that receive your data, there are no accounts, and we do not collect, transmit or sell personal information. You can turn history off or clear it in Settings, or delete everything by uninstalling the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising ID and similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance, and may collect device information such as IP address, device identifiers and diagnostic data. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), you are asked for consent, and you can change your choice later in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.

See how Google uses data when you use partner sites or apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

Links and actions

When you tap an action such as "Open link", "Call" or "Search", the scanned content is passed to the app you choose (browser, phone, email, maps). Always check a link before opening it. We are not responsible for the content of third-party sites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page.
